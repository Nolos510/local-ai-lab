# Resume Bullets

Use this skill to turn work evidence into strong resume-ready bullets.

## Workflow

1. Identify the role, target audience, and relevant accomplishment.
2. Extract action, scope, technical depth, and measurable impact.
3. Prefer specific evidence over generic claims.
4. Produce multiple variants when tone or seniority is uncertain.
5. Keep bullets truthful, compact, and easy to defend in an interview.

## Output

Use `templates/bullets.md` for reusable resume evidence notes.
