# SEO Audit

Use this skill for practical, prioritized SEO review.

## Workflow

1. Identify the page, audience, target query, and business goal.
2. Check title, meta description, headings, internal links, content depth, indexability, and performance signals when available.
3. Separate critical technical blockers from content improvements.
4. Prioritize recommendations by likely impact and effort.
5. Keep the output implementation-oriented.

## Output

Use `templates/audit.md` when the audit should be saved or repeated.
