# Research Brief

Use this skill to synthesize research into a decision-ready brief.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the research question and audience.
2. Gather source material from the repo, user-provided notes, or approved search.
3. Separate confirmed facts, interpretations, and unknowns.
4. Prefer concise synthesis over source-by-source summaries.
5. End with implications, risks, and next research steps.

## Output

Use `templates/brief.md` for briefs that should be saved or reused.
