# Local AI Lab 30/60/90-Day Readiness Roadmap

## Days 0-30: Trustworthy Daily Driver

Readiness work:

- Independently review and human-confirm, reject, or retire every real draft.
- Rerun or retire the five unscored real artifacts that currently have no raw
  response records.
- Capture throughput and peak RAM for every efficiency-frontier candidate.
- Persist model role and provenance; keep embedding/reranker models in their own
  lane.
- Add one cockpit health summary for provider, judge, reviewer, Qdrant, runtime
  inventory, and pending evidence work.
- Complete keyboard, 1440x900, 1024x768, and 390x844 audits of all core pages.
- Publish one sanitized end-to-end model decision case study.

Optional innovation: none. Trust and workflow closure own this period.

Target: no P0 blocker, every criterion at least 4.0/5, and score at least 84.

## Days 31-60: Recovery And Evaluation Breadth

Readiness work:

- Demonstrate interrupted batch cleanup, retry, and duplicate-safe import.
- Add retrieval-specific evaluation for embedding and reranker models.
- Add row-level evidence completeness and direct remediation.
- Replace dense mobile tables with summary/detail presentation where needed.
- Extend the completed warmed-route and idle-process baseline with cold startup,
  active-batch CPU/RAM, queue latency, and model-load churn measurements.
- Validate a fresh-machine five-minute demo path and a truthful full-runtime
  setup path.

Optional innovation:

- Thin workload-aware portfolio recommendation experiment using confirmed
  evidence only.

Target: utility, trust, reliability, and privacy at least 4.5/5; score at least
89.

## Days 61-90: Release Proof And One Differentiator

Readiness work:

- Run a clean capture-to-confirm cycle on both LM Studio and Ollama where local
  models are available.
- Export and inspect the portfolio report for path, prompt, response, and token
  leakage.
- Have a reviewer follow the setup and case study without developer guidance.
- Freeze a versioned release scorecard, known limitations, recovery runbook, and
  portfolio screenshots.

Optional innovation:

- Choose exactly one: evidence completeness, resource-aware batch ordering, or
  multi-judge disagreement analysis.
- Ship it only if it improves measured utility without weakening human score
  authority or local-first boundaries.

Target: at least 92/100, no P0 or privacy/data-integrity blocker, no criterion
below 4.0/5, all protected criteria at least 4.5/5, and all eight journeys pass.
